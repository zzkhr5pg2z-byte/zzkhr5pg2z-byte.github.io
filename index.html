Forward Operating Base Kestrel

Iron Perimeter

The base's security robots have turned on the garrison. You're the last one inside the wire. Use the buildings: three floors of HQ, the barracks balconies, the guard towers, the hangar catwalk and the container stacks.

Crawlers rush and bite. Sentinels carry rifles, take cover and peek out to shoot; some carry a frag for when you go to ground. A chirp means one has just spotted you, and it tells the others near it. They look where they're facing, so you can slip past one looking the other way, but they hear gunfire (the suppressed SMG barely carries) and come to look. Gunners walk at you with miniguns. Marksmen snipe from rooftops, sweeping their sights across the base; you'll see their red laser before they fire. Drones and Brutes join later.

Frags bounce and blow apart groups; hold the key to cook one so it goes off sooner. Smoke hides you, so use it to cross open ground. Flashbangs blind every robot that sees the bang (and you, if you're looking). EMP freezes robots and drops drones. Armour plates soak up damage, medkits heal 60 and a stim makes you faster and tougher for 12 seconds.

Kill streaks count robots you take down without dropping under 35 health. 3 puts every robot on your map for 15 s (otherwise it only shows the ones you can see or hear firing). 5 drops a supply crate on a parachute. 8 earns an airstrike: aim at open ground and press B, then get clear. Supply drops also come in during the fight: follow the red smoke and hold E to open them.

Cover isn't all equal: rifle, LMG, DMR and sniper rounds go through plasterboard walls, wood and the hangar's sheet metal (weaker for it, and the robots' rounds do the same); pistol, SMG and shotgun rounds don't. Concrete, sandbags, HESCO and containers stop everything.

Mortars: from wave 4 the robots sometimes call a barrage down on where you are. Each round is marked by a red flare a couple of seconds before it lands; move.

Fuel burns: shoot the tanker by the fuel point, the bulk tanks inside it or the red drums beside some of the cover and they'll spray fire for a moment, then explode. Stand well back.

Training range: targets at 10 to 60 m down the main road, one walking across, with every gun and unlimited ammo. Press Esc to see your hits and leave.

Head and visor shots do double damage. Green crates refill ammo and top up frags and armour. Robots drop supplies.

Last games
Key bindings

Click an action, then press its new key (Esc to cancel). A key already in use swaps over. The arrow keys always move; Esc, P, Tab and the number keys stay as they are.

Play with a friend

PvP loads you both into the hub in the hangar. At the match terminal (E, or Tab anywhere) the host picks Gun Game (a new gun after every kill, first to the kill target wins), Duel rounds, Scavenger (everyone starts with a pistol; grab the guns lying round the base) or King of the Hill (hold the glowing zone on your own to score; it moves every 45 s), and switches on no recoil, no camping, unlimited ammo or one-shot kills. Spawns are random and out of sight. Co-op: the two of you against the robot waves; hold E by a downed friend to revive them.
