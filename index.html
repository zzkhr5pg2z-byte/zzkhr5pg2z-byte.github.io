    Online modes

    Up to ten players: one hosts, the rest join with the code. PvP (everyone against everyone) loads you all into the hub. At the match terminal (E, or Tab anywhere) the host picks Gun Game (a new gun after every kill, first to the kill target wins), Duel rounds (one life each, last one standing takes the round, best of three by default; two of you start in opposite halves and swap every round, more are spread round the map), Scavenger (everyone starts with a pistol; grab the guns lying round the base) or King of the Hill (hold the glowing zone on your own to score; it moves every 45 s) or Team Deathmatch (Blue against Red, up to five a side; no friendly fire, your team on your map and named over their heads, first team to the kill target), and switches on no recoil, no camping, unlimited ammo or one-shot kills. A kill goes to whoever last hit you; after a respawn you have two seconds of protection (until you fire). In Team Deathmatch everyone wears their team's colour, a band round the chest and arms. Hold Tab during a match for the scoreboard. In co-op and Team Deathmatch, press N to ping: it marks where you're aiming, or the enemy you're aiming at, for your side to see. Other spawns are random and out of sight. Co-op: all of you against the robot waves, which grow with every extra player; hold E by a downed teammate to revive them. Someone joining part way through comes straight in (in a duel, at the next round).

    The robots

    Crawlers rush and bite. Sentinels carry rifles, take cover and peek out to shoot; some carry a frag for when you go to ground. A chirp means one has just spotted you, and it tells the others near it. They look where they're facing, so you can slip past one looking the other way, but they hear gunfire (the suppressed SMG barely carries) and come to look. They work as a squad: some swing wide to come at you from the side, they keep firing at where you ducked out of sight for a moment, one badly hurt falls back to cover further off, and if they hear you reload close by they may rush you. Gunners walk at you with miniguns. Marksmen snipe from rooftops, sweeping their sights across the base; you'll see their red laser before they fire. Drones and Brutes join later.

    Grenades

    Frags bounce and blow apart groups; hold the key to cook one so it goes off sooner. Smoke hides you, so use it to cross open ground. Flashbangs blind every robot that sees the bang (and you, if you're looking). EMP freezes robots and drops drones. Armour plates soak up damage, medkits heal 60 and a stim makes you faster and tougher for 12 seconds.

    Kill streaks

    Kill streaks count robots you take down without dropping under 35 health. 3 puts every robot on your map for 15 s (otherwise it only shows the ones you can see or hear firing). 5 drops a supply crate on a parachute. 8 earns an airstrike: aim at open ground and press B, then get clear. Supply drops also come in during the fight: follow the red smoke and hold E to open them.

    Cover and penetration

    Cover isn't all equal: rifle, LMG, DMR and sniper rounds go through plasterboard walls, wood and the hangar's sheet metal (weaker for it, and the robots' rounds do the same); pistol, SMG and shotgun rounds don't. Concrete, sandbags, HESCO and containers stop everything.

    Patrols and the alarm

    Patrols and the alarm: each wave starts with a garrison already out on patrol round the base, somewhere different every time, and they haven't seen you yet. One that catches sight of you takes a moment to make you out; stay out of its view and it walks on. They go to look at gunfire they hear. Once one has you (or you shoot one, or a minute or so goes by and the base's sensors find you) the alarm sounds, every robot is told roughly where you are and the rest of the wave comes in. When they lose you they search where they last saw or heard you. A robot on patrol hit by the suppressed SMG takes a second or so to get on the radio: drop it before then and the alarm stays quiet, though others close by may hear the shots and come to look.

    Objectives

    Objectives: each wave has one, marked in blue on screen and on the map. Recover the intel (a case somewhere indoors; hold E by it) shows every robot on your map for 25 s. Destroy the comms uplink (shoot or blow up the mast) stops it calling in two more robots when the alarm goes. Hold the supply cache (25 s inside its ring, in total) refills your ammo, armour and frags. Eliminate the commander (the robot with a red beacon) takes away its squad's sharper aim. Each pays points; the wave ends when its robots are down either way. Every fifth wave is an Extraction: a Blackhawk flies in to a landing zone out in the open and waits 40 s on the ground. Get into the LZ while it's down and the run ends as a win, with bonus points and half as much experience again; stay and fight and it leaves without you.

    Radio chatter

    Radio chatter: robots in sight call out on their radios, a squawk and a word over their heads: Contact (it's seen you), Reloading (push now), Flanking, Suppressing, Frag out, Unit down. Jammed, they can't.

    Missions

    Missions (on your own, or hosting co-op: pick one under Mission before deploying or hosting): one garrison to get through and a few objectives in order, then out on the chopper. Intel run: take the intel, get out. Comms blackout: bring down the uplink, hold the supply cache, get out. Decapitation: find the commander, take it down, get out. Aboard the chopper it's complete; killed, it's failed. You start with a rifle, an SMG and a shotgun (and a marksman's rifle for the commander), two frags, a plate and a medkit.

    Mid-wave events

    Mid-wave events: from wave 2, some waves bring something with them partway in. Jamming: for about half a minute your map and radar go to static and the compass wanders, but their radios are down too, so a robot that spots you can't call the others or raise the alarm until it lifts. Fuel fire: a fuel drum or tank catches a stray round, burns and goes up, and thick smoke drifts off it for most of a minute; nobody sees through it. Mortars (on your own, from wave 4): the robots call a barrage down on where you are. Each round is marked by a red flare a couple of seconds before it lands; move. Power cut: for 40 s the base's lamps and ceiling lights go out, and with them its sensors and the PA, so it won't find you on its own or sound the siren (its robots still have their radios).

    Fuel and explosions

    Fuel burns: shoot the tanker by the fuel point, the bulk tanks inside it or the red drums beside some of the cover and they'll spray fire for a moment, then explode. Stand well back.

    Training range

    Training range: targets at 10 to 60 m down the main road, one walking across, with every gun and unlimited ammo. Press Esc to see your hits and leave.

    The Killhouse map

    Killhouse (pick it under Map): a walled training compound built for fighting at close quarters. A shoot house of plywood rooms open to the roof, with an instructors' gallery looking down into them and a bridge across to a door high in the outside wall; an office block and a barracks across a lane; a street of flat-roofed houses, two with stairs to the roof; a maze of stacked containers; and the courtyard inside the gate, where the chopper comes in for extraction. Rounds go through the plywood and the plaster. Online, you both play the host's map.
